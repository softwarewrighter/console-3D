# New procedural equipment previews

The local index is `/new-models.html`. Individual pages are
`/equipment.html?model=3279`, `029`, `cor24`, and `macbook`. The existing server
binds to `0.0.0.0:5803`. These pages serve reviewable first-pass candidates, not
historically verified production assets. Source controls distinguish our render,
our compact GLB, original photograph, and supplied detailed reconstruction.

Original photos are copied without modification into `references/`. Supplied GLBs
remain local and are ignored by Git. Photographer, license and reconstruction
origin are not established. Geometry is generated by X_eTaL's Bpy helpers plus
`scripts/equipment_scene.py`, using the low-level primitives from the shared
construction modules. No source-mesh geometry is extracted into our models.
Small deterministic artwork is generated by `scripts/make_equipment_decals.py`.

## Rebuild and publish

```sh
uv run scripts/make_equipment_decals.py
python3 scripts/build.py 3279 --device OPTIX --candidate-only
python3 scripts/build.py 029 --device OPTIX --candidate-only
python3 scripts/build.py cor24 --device OPTIX --candidate-only
python3 scripts/build.py macbook --device OPTIX --candidate-only
python3 scripts/publish_equipment_previews.py
just serve
```

The publisher copies only candidates with completed reports into `work/preview/new/`
and retains candidate directories and previous renders for comparison. The four
pages use Lucy's one shared keyboard handler. Initial display is a still render;
interactive rendering starts only when selected. Switching back to a still hides
the 3D viewer and disables camera auto-rotation. Raw detailed references use their
own framing because their normalized reconstruction coordinates are not physical
model coordinates.

## Review and validation

First renders exposed a 029 keyboard occlusion and an excessive laptop key height.
The 029 deck and keys were moved onto the actual casing slope. Laptop key height
was reduced from 9 mm to 0.9 mm and framing enlarged to include the lid. Corrected
candidates are retained separately from the initial versions.

OptiX builds use factory startup, bounded execution, checked fresh Blender/GLB/OBJ
artifacts, six fixed orthographic views plus two perspective views, and independent
GLB semantic-name/bounds round trips. Lucy CPU diagnostics are stored under
`work/*-cpu-check/` and source diagnostics under `work/*-reference-check/`.
`python3 scripts/check_equipment_previews.py` passed all four pages: stills, compact
GLB loading, zoom controls, photo switching, source GLBs, and mobile width. Reports
and screenshots remain in `work/equipment-browser-check/`.

These checks establish a working rendering pipeline and reviewable geometry.
They do not establish historical fidelity. In particular the 029 transport wells,
3279 keyboard/cabinet proportions, board electrical layout and MacBook variant
remain significant refinement targets. The existing five accepted model outputs
are preserved; these four live in a separate candidate-preview index.
