# Compact equipment reconstruction plan

## Scope

Refine the IBM 1053 printer assembly using the supplied full-console GLB and the
original photographs. Retain the indicator display and keyboard only in the
reference viewer, not in the compact production model. Keep the 2741 separate.

## Evidence and inherited workflow

- Lucy owns the shared camera controls, bounded process runner, and canonical
  `less-wrong-3d` skill. Its survey distinguishes diagnostic success from fidelity.
- The 5100's `docs/less-wrong.md` establishes feature queues, camera matching,
  source-local geometry, rollback, and the failed-hypothesis rule.
- The 1130 uses semantic components and named cameras. Its geometry is evidence
  to inspect, not a dimensional authority.
- `less-worse.md` was not found in the neighboring repositories or home-directory
  search at task start. This repository's reusable feedback-loop document will
  use that requested name and link back to the existing canonical skill.

## Initial measurable targets

1. Match printer height/width and shoulder depth/width ratios within roughly 5%
   of the explicitly chosen source-model landmarks. This measures agreement with
   that reconstruction, not verified historical dimensions.
2. Align the switch row, scale, and three blue controls with photographed fascia
   landmarks. Preserve the sticker correction and sixteen switches.
3. Keep the final asset under 25,000 triangles, 60 mesh nodes, and 700 KiB where
   feasible; compare actual GLB size, triangle count, and runtime draw calls.
4. Preserve front, both obliques, top, and rear views per accepted candidate.
   Never claim a numeric fidelity score based only on export or a single view.

The last passing output remains available until the next candidate has complete
artifacts and a visual review. Record rejected hypotheses as well as successes.
